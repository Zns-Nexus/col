# Col brand directions

Files: `col-index-logo.png`, `col-index-brand-kit.png`, `col-fold-logo.png`, `col-fold-brand-kit.png`.

Two monochrome concepts for review. These assets do not replace the live website identity.

- **Index:** stacked catalog tabs. Rounded typography, ordered spacing, and a compact silhouette.
- **Fold:** a continuous folded strip reduced into a C. Diagonal cuts, architectural geometry, and a tactile editorial system.

Each direction includes a transparent black logo lockup and a 3 × 3 brand-kit board. Boards show proposed applications, not working product screens. Images are raster concepts generated with the built-in imagegen tool using the brandkit skill. Production vector artwork would follow selection of a direction.

## Logo prompts

### Index

Generate ONE premium new logo for Col, a curated directory of UI libraries used by developers and product designers. Output a crisp wide horizontal logo lockup on a genuinely transparent background, no mockup. Flat pure black ink only. Left symbol: a highly reduced capital C constructed from three tightly stacked catalog index tabs, one compact unified silhouette with an open right side, a restrained staggered termination on the right suggesting a curated stack. Strong negative space, warm small corner radii, disciplined equal stroke widths, optically balanced. Must be immediately readable at favicon scale, not a literal folder illustration or three disconnected bars, not the incumbent linked two-piece symbol. Right wordmark: exact text 'Col', custom rounded geometric sans serif with a generous open C, circular o, restrained l terminal, medium-bold weight. Match symbol and wordmark height optically. Refined spacing with wide breathing room. Brand metaphor is collected choices ordered into one place. Bauhaus reduction meets contemporary developer editorial identity. Beautiful balanced original identity, professional flat vector-like contours, no dimensional effects, shadow, bevel, metallic texture, construction grid, caption, slogan, border, app tile or extra variants. One logo only. Wide 2:1 landscape composition, logo occupying about 70% of width with generous safe margin.

### Fold

Generate ONE premium new logo for Col, a curated directory of UI libraries for developers and product designers. Output a crisp wide horizontal logo lockup on a genuinely transparent background, no mockup. Flat pure black ink only. Left symbol: an elegant compact folded-strip capital C, a continuous wide band that makes three sides of a softly squared C with two asymmetric diagonal return cuts at the open right edge. Express collection and discovery as a folded catalog page. The diagonal cuts and generous central negative space are the signature, like an architectural paper-fold reduced to a precise two-dimensional silhouette. Single contiguous shape, readable at 16 pixels, disciplined subtle corner radii, balanced mass, open not closed, no cube, hexagon, generic ring or chain link, no imitation famous mark. Right wordmark exact 'Col' in a bespoke clean rounded sans serif, bold but calm, matching the geometric cut logic with a subtle diagonal terminal on l, excellent kerning. Meaning is many resources folded into one clear destination. Contemporary independent design studio, architectural clarity with tactile editorial character. Different from a stacked-tab logo: one flowing connected band with diagonal incisions. No text other than Col. No shadows, bevels, chrome, gradient, grayscale texture, construction lines, caption, frame, app tile or variants. Flat original identity. One logo only. Wide 2:1 landscape composition, lockup at 70% of image width with generous safe margin.
